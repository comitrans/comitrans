One message is enough. You tell me when and where from, I reply with the price and the pickup time.

Your address Airport

To the airport

  1. Send the details

    Date, flight time, address and number of passengers. A call, WhatsApp or Viber all work.

  2. Get a confirmation

    I reply with the price and the pickup time, allowing for traffic and check-in.

  3. I pick you up

    At your address, at the agreed time. Luggage goes in the boot, you take your seat.

  4. You arrive at the terminal

    I drop you at the departures entrance.

From the airport

  1. Send your flight number

    Along with the date, the address and the number of passengers.

  2. I follow your flight

    I time the pickup to the actual landing, so a late plane is not your problem.

  3. I wait at arrivals

    Message me once you have your bags and we meet outside the exit.

  4. I drive you to your door

    To your address in Novi Sad or nearby.

Questions and answers

How do I book a ride?

Call, or message me on WhatsApp or Viber. The quickest way is the form at the bottom of this page: fill in the details and the message writes itself.

How far ahead should I book?

The earlier the better, especially for early morning flights and weekends. Do get in touch at the last minute too: if I am free, I drive.

What if my flight is late?

Send me your flight number. I follow the landing time and arrive when the plane actually lands.

How much luggage fits?

The boot is large. If you are bringing more than the usual luggage, let me know in advance.

How do I pay?

We agree the price before the ride, and you pay when we set off.

Do you drive at night and on holidays?

Yes. Flights do not keep office hours, and neither do I.

Do you drive to other cities and airports?

Yes. If your route is not listed, tell me where from and where to and I will reply with a price.
